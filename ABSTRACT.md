ProductManager is a roadmap planning tool that grows into a Monte Carlo market-simulation
decision-support system: plan initiatives/projects/teams on the execution side, model markets
(segments, firms, coverage, opportunities) on the market side, and simulate roadmap scenarios to
compare projected bookings/ARR/NPV. Backend is C#/.NET (Domain, Engine, Api, Cli); frontend is
Next.js. See `docs/PRDs/product_requirements.md` for the authoritative product spec.
